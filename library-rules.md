# Library Business Rules

**Course:** MSAI-5523 — Language of Computation, Module 2 Assignment (Part 2)
**Author:** gwcarlson1@mail.lipscomb.edu

These are my decisions as the library owner for how my library operates.

## 1. Books

Track each book's title, ISBN, publication year, and author or authors. A book may have multiple authors.

## 2. Physical Copies

A book may have multiple physical copies. Each copy needs its own unique ID and condition. Track whether a copy is available, checked out, or lost.

## 3. Members

Track member ID, name, email address, and membership status (active or suspended).

## 4. Borrowing Limit

Members may borrow a maximum of three books at one time.

## 5. Borrowing Period

Books are due 14 days after checkout.

## 6. Availability

The same physical copy cannot be checked out to two people simultaneously.

## 7. Overdue Books

A book is overdue when its due date has passed and it has not been returned.

## 8. Lost Books

Keep lost books in inventory records, but do not allow them to be checked out.

## 9. Suspended Members

Suspended members cannot borrow additional books.

## 10. Borrowing History

Keep a record of checkout dates, due dates, and return dates.
